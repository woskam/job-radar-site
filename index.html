How it works

1. Search or browse

Filter by keyword, category, segment, and location across every listing 12GetAJob tracks.

2. Save it as an alert

One click carries your filters over -- add an email, confirm, done.

3. Get a daily email

Only new matches since the last one. Unsubscribe any time, one click, no login.

Open source underneath

12GetAJob is built on Job Radar, a self-hosted job-search pipeline, and Job Radar Hub, its public API and MCP server. Both are open source, and you can self-host the whole thing yourself.
